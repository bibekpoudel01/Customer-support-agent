Failed Payments

Q My payment failed but I want to complete my order.
  1. Confirm card/UPI/net-banking details are correct and not expired.
  2. Ask the customer to check if their bank/card issuer blocked the transaction (common with international cards or first-time large purchases).
  3. Suggest an alternate payment method (different card, UPI app, net banking, or COD if available).
  4. If the same failure repeats across multiple methods, it may be a gateway/server-side issue — escalate to technical/payment gateway team.

Charged But Order Not Placed / Missing

Q Money was deducted from my account but I don't see an order confirmation.

This is one of the most common and highest-priority payment issues. Steps:

  1. Ask for the exact amount deducted, date/time, and last 4 digits of card or UPI transaction ID (do not ask for full card number, CVV, or OTP).
  2. Check the order history for any order in "Payment Pending" or "Failed" status matching that amount/time.
  3. If no matching order exists, this is a failed transaction where the bank debited but the merchant did not receive confirmation — very common with UPI due to timeout issues.
  4. These amounts are auto-reversed by the bank within 5-7 business days if no order was created on our end. Provide the customer this timeline.
  5. If 7 business days pass with no reversal, request the bank reference number (UTR/RRN) and escalate to the payments team for manual reconciliation.
⚠ Fraud prevention Never manually create the order or promise a duplicate refund without escalation verification — this prevents double-refund fraud.

Double Charged

Q I was charged twice for the same order.
  1. Confirm both transactions in the customer's bank statement (transaction ID, amount, time).
  2. Check if two separate orders were created, or if it's a single order with a duplicate charge attempt (common when a customer clicks "Pay" multiple times during a slow load).
  3. If duplicate order: cancel one order immediately (before it ships) and initiate refund for that transaction — refer to Return & Refund refund timelines.
  4. If single order with duplicate charge (no second order exists): escalate to payments team with both transaction IDs for reversal of the extra charge.

Cash on Delivery (COD) Issues

Q Why was COD not available for my order?

COD may be disabled due to:

  • Order value exceeding the COD limit (typically capped, e.g., high-value large appliances).
  • Delivery pin code not serviceable for COD.
  • Customer's account flagged for previous COD refusals (multiple refused deliveries can restrict COD eligibility for 90 days).
Q I paid the delivery agent in cash but the app still shows "Payment Pending."
  1. Ask for the delivery date and courier receipt/reference if available.
  2. COD payment confirmation from the courier partner can take 24-48 hours to reflect in the system.
  3. If still showing pending after 48 hours, escalate to payments team with delivery date and order ID.
Q Can I pay part cash, part online for COD orders?

This depends on platform capability — if not supported, advise customer to choose one payment method at checkout. Currently our system does not support split payment.

Refund to Wrong / Closed Account

Q My refund was sent to a card I no longer use / a bank account that's closed.

Refunds to a closed account are typically auto-reversed by the bank back to us within 7-10 business days, after which we can reissue to an active account or as store credit. Ask the customer to share updated bank/UPI details in the order's refund request form (do not collect this via chat/email directly — always route to secure in-app forms).

Security Note

🔒 Never request sensitive data Agents must never ask customers for: full card number, CVV, OTP, net banking password, or PIN. Only transaction ID, last 4 digits, amount, and date/time are needed for investigation.

Escalation Trigger for This Category

Escalate to a human agent (Payments Team) if:

  • Amount debited with no order created, unresolved after 7 business days.
  • Suspected duplicate charge requiring manual reversal.
  • COD payment confirmation missing after 48 hours.
  • Any case requiring bank-side reconciliation (UTR/RRN lookups).
