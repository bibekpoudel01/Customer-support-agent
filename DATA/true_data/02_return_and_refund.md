Return & Refund Knowledge Base — Active Inventory Rules
This document outlines the return and refund rules specific to our current catalog of 10 active products. Use this to determine return eligibility, condition requirements, and whether an item qualifies for a refund vs. a replacement.

1. Electronics & Computing
Q: Can I return the Redmi Note 13 smartphone?
A: Yes, within 7 days, provided:

There is no visible physical damage (scratches, cracks, liquid exposure).

The IMEI/serial number matches the original invoice exactly.

The device has been factory-reset by the customer (to remove Google/Mi accounts and lock screens).

All original accessories (Xiaomi charger, cable, SIM tool, box) are included.
Note: A device that shows signs of drops or unauthorized repairs is instantly rejected. Defective out-of-the-box (DOA) units qualify for an immediate replacement.

Q: Can I return the Fantech Fighter Mechanical Keyboard?
A: Yes, within the standard 7-day window if unused and undamaged. Keyboards showing heavy wear (scratched keycaps, food crumbs inside) from extended use will be rejected as "used." Individual malfunctioning keys discovered within 7 days qualify as a defect claim (replacement).

2. Audio & Wearables (Hygiene Sensitive)
Q: Can I return the boAt Rockerz 450 Headphones?
A: Only if unopened and unused, due to hygiene reasons (worn on the ears/head). If the seal is broken but the product is defective (won't pair, one side dead, no sound), this qualifies as "Defective on Arrival" and is eligible for replacement. General "change of mind" or "I don't like the sound" returns are rejected if the box is opened.

3. Home & Kitchen (Small Appliances)
Q: Can I return the Baltra Electric Rice Cooker or CG Table Fan if I change my mind?
A: Yes, within 7 days, provided the item is unused.

For the Rice Cooker: It must be uncooked-in, free of water stains, rice residue, or scratches on the non-stick pot. Used cookers are rejected for change-of-mind, though genuine heating defects qualify for replacement.

For the Fan: Must be clean, un-dented, and packed with all original manuals and styrofoam.

4. Large Appliances
Q: Can I return the LG 260L Refrigerator for a refund?
A: No. Large appliances are on a 10-day replacement-only window strictly for manufacturing defects (e.g., compressor not working, arriving with deep dents). Change-of-mind returns are not accepted once delivered due to heavy reverse-logistics costs.
Note: If a customer reports a cooling defect after 10 days, route them to the Warranty & Repair process for an LG technician visit.

5. Books
Q: Can I return the Bestseller Book Set of 4?
A: Yes, within 7 days if unread and undamaged. Books with bent spines, folded pages, or coffee stains will be rejected. If the customer received the set but one book is missing or has printing errors (blank pages), process this as a replacement for the defective/missing item, not a standard return.

6. Groceries & Perishables (Non-Returnable)
Q: Can a customer return DDC Standard Milk, Wai Wai Noodles, or Kurkure Masala Munch?
A: No. Food and dairy items are strictly non-returnable for "change of mind" due to health and safety regulations.
Exceptions (Quality/Spoilage Issues):

DDC Milk: If delivered curdled, spoiled, or in a leaking packet, a full refund or replacement credit must be issued immediately. Must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.

Wai Wai / Kurkure: If the packets arrive torn, open, pest-damaged, or expired upon delivery, issue a refund/replacement. Must be reported within 3 days of delivery.
