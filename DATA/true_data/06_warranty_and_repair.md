# Warranty & Repair Knowledge Base

## Warranty Periods by Category

**Q: What is the warranty period for my product?**
A: Standard warranty terms (unless otherwise stated on the product page):
- **Small electronics** (earbuds, Bluetooth speakers, power banks, cables): 6 months
- **Personal care appliances** (trimmer, hair dryer, electric toothbrush): 1 year
- **Kitchen appliances** (air fryer, kettle, mixer grinder, microwave, induction cooktop): 1-2 years
- **Large appliances** (refrigerator, washing machine, split AC): 1 year on the unit, **10 years on the compressor/motor** (compressor-only coverage after year 1)
- **TVs and laptops**: 1 year, extendable via paid extended warranty plans
- **Furniture and mattresses**: 1-3 years depending on product (frame/structure only; fabric/upholstery typically excluded after 6 months)

**Q: What does the warranty cover?**
A: Manufacturing defects, functional failures under normal use (e.g., motor failure, circuit faults, non-functioning display). It does **not** cover:
- Physical damage from drops, water damage (unless product is rated water-resistant and used within spec), or unauthorized repair/modification
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., fading fabric, worn cushioning)
- Damage from using incompatible accessories or incorrect voltage/power source
- Cosmetic issues that don't affect function

**Q: Where can I find my product's exact warranty period and start date?**
A: Warranty starts from the **delivery date**, visible in Orders > Order Details > "Warranty Info," or on the physical warranty card included in the box.

## Filing a Warranty Claim

**Q: My product stopped working within the warranty period. What do I do?**
A:
1. Confirm the product is still within its warranty window (check delivery date).
2. Ask the customer to describe the issue and, if possible, provide a photo/video of the problem.
3. Rule out simple fixes first (e.g., power source issues, incorrect setup) before filing a claim — see Technical Support document for basic troubleshooting.
4. If genuinely defective, initiate a warranty claim: Orders > select product > "Warranty Claim/Repair Request."
5. Choose repair pickup (technician visit for large appliances) or ship-in repair (small electronics, mailed to service center).

**Q: How long does a repair take?**
A: 
- **Small electronics** (ship-in repair): 5-10 business days after receipt at service center.
- **Large appliances** (in-home technician visit): technician typically scheduled within 2-4 business days of claim, repair completed same visit or with a follow-up if parts need ordering (add 3-7 days for parts).

**Q: Will I get a replacement instead of a repair?**
A: If the same defect recurs after one repair attempt, or if the product is deemed unrepairable, the customer is eligible for a full replacement or refund. First-time issues are generally repaired, not replaced, unless the product is a large appliance within the first 10 days (see Return & Refund policy replacement-only window).

## Out-of-Warranty Repairs

**Q: My product is out of warranty. Can I still get it repaired?**
A: Yes, paid out-of-warranty repair service is available for most large appliances and select electronics. Cost estimate is provided after technician diagnosis; the customer can approve or decline before repair proceeds. Diagnosis visit fee may apply even if the customer declines repair.

## Extended Warranty

**Q: Can I purchase extended warranty after buying the product?**
A: Extended warranty plans must typically be purchased **within 30 days of delivery**. They are not available for purchase after this window or after the standard warranty has already expired.

**Q: How do I claim under extended warranty?**
A: Same process as a standard warranty claim — the system automatically checks if the extended plan is active on the account and applies the correct coverage.

## Escalation Trigger for This Category
Escalate to a human agent if:
- Customer disputes a claim rejection (e.g., claims damage was not their fault).
- Repair has been delayed beyond stated timelines.
- Recurring defect after repair — replacement/refund eligibility dispute.
- Extended warranty purchase/claim discrepancy.
