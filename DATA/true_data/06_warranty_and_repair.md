Warranty Periods by Product
Q: What is the warranty period for my product?
A: Warranty terms for our active catalog start from the delivery date:

LG 260L Double Door Refrigerator: 1 year comprehensive on the unit, 10 years on the Smart Inverter Compressor (compressor-only coverage after year 1).

Redmi Note 13: 1 year on the smartphone, 6 months on the in-box charger and cable.

Electronics & Small Appliances (Fantech Keyboard, boAt Headphones, CG Table Fan, Baltra Rice Cooker): 1 year comprehensive warranty.

Consumables & Media (DDC Milk, Wai Wai, Kurkure, Book Set): No warranty. These items do not have repair warranties. If a customer reports a quality issue, refer strictly to the Return & Refund document for spoilage/defect windows (e.g., 24 hours for milk).

Q: What does the warranty cover?
A: It covers manufacturing defects and functional failures under normal use (e.g., the CG Fan motor fails, the Baltra Cooker stops heating, the boAt headphones lose Bluetooth connectivity).
It does not cover:

Physical damage from drops or mishandling (e.g., a cracked Redmi screen, a dented LG fridge door).

Liquid damage (unless the product is specifically rated for it and used within spec).

Damage from electrical surges or using non-original chargers.

Normal wear and tear (e.g., worn-out ear cushions on the boAt headphones, faded keycaps on the Fantech keyboard).

Q: Where can I find my product's exact warranty start date?
A: Warranty starts from the delivery date, visible in Orders > Order Details > "Warranty Info," or validated via the original invoice and physical warranty card included in the box.

Filing a Warranty Claim
Q: My product stopped working within the warranty period. What do I do?
A:

Confirm the product is still within its warranty window (check delivery date).

Rule out simple fixes first using the Technical Support document (e.g., ensure the Fantech keyboard isn't plugged into a dead USB port; check if the LG fridge thermostat is set correctly).

If genuinely defective, ask the customer to provide a photo/video of the problem if applicable (e.g., error codes).

Initiate a warranty claim: Orders > select product > "Warranty Claim/Repair Request."

Route based on product type:

LG Refrigerator: Schedule an in-home technician visit.

Redmi, Fantech, boAt, CG Fan, Baltra Cooker: Arrange a ship-in repair or direct the customer to the nearest authorized service center drop-off.

Q: How long does a repair take?
A:

In-home visits (LG Refrigerator): Technician is typically scheduled within 2-4 business days of the claim. The repair is completed on the same visit unless parts need ordering (add 3-7 days for parts).

Ship-in repairs (Phones, Peripherals, Small Appliances): 5-10 business days after the item is received at the service center.

Q: Will I get a replacement instead of a repair?
A: First-time issues are generally repaired, not replaced. However, if the product is deemed unrepairable by the service center, or if the same exact defect recurs after one repair attempt, the customer is eligible for a full replacement. (Note: DOA or defects reported within the first 7-10 days fall under the Return & Refund replacement policy, not standard repair).

Out-of-Warranty Service
Q: My product is out of warranty. Can I still get it repaired?
A: Yes, paid out-of-warranty repair service is available specifically for the LG Refrigerator and Redmi Note 13. A cost estimate is provided after technician diagnosis; the customer can approve or decline before the repair proceeds. A diagnosis/visit fee applies even if the customer declines the repair. (Note: Out-of-warranty repairs are generally not offered for low-cost items like the boAt headphones or Fantech keyboard).
