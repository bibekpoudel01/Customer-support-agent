Technical Support Knowledge Base — Active Inventory Troubleshooting
This document outlines product-specific troubleshooting for our current active catalog of 10 items. Use this when a customer's issue relates to a physical product malfunction, quality complaint, or missing item.

1. Electronics & Accessories
Q: My Redmi Note 13 is running slow, overheating, or the battery drains fast.
A:

Check for pending Android/MIUI OS and app updates.

Close background apps and restart the device.

Overheating during charging is often linked to using a non-original adapter. Recommend using the original Xiaomi fast charger and cable.

If battery health is severely degraded or the phone fails to hold a charge within the warranty period, file a warranty claim.

Q: My boAt Rockerz 450 headphones won't pair, audio cuts out, or they won't charge.
A:

Pairing failures: Ensure the headphones are in pairing mode (LED flashing), forget/remove "boAt Rockerz 450" from the phone's Bluetooth list, and re-pair from scratch.

Audio drops: Intermittent connection drops are often caused by distance/interference. Recommend staying within 5-8 meters of the source device.

Won't charge: Try an alternate Micro-USB/Type-C cable and adapter. If the LED charging indicator does not light up at all, escalate for a warranty claim.

Q: My Fantech Fighter Mechanical Keyboard isn't typing, specific keys are dead, or the RGB lights are off.
A:

Not recognized: Ensure the USB cable is plugged directly into the PC's motherboard or a direct laptop port, not through a unpowered USB hub.

RGB off: Check the keyboard manual for the specific shortcut key combination to toggle the backlight on/off (usually FN + a function key).

Dead keys: For mechanical keyboards with unresponsive individual keys, this typically indicates a hardware switch fault. File a warranty claim rather than attempting DIY repair.

2. Home & Kitchen Appliances
Q: My LG 260L Double Door Refrigerator isn't cooling properly.
A:

Check that the thermostat/temperature dial inside the fridge is set correctly (not accidentally turned to minimum or "winter" mode).

Ensure the door seal isn't damaged or obstructed by food items preventing a full magnetic seal.

Confirm adequate clearance (at least 5-10cm) behind and around the unit for ventilation.

If cooling issues persist after these checks, this is a compressor/refrigerant issue. File a warranty claim (note: LG compressors usually carry a 10-year warranty).

Q: My Baltra Electric Rice Cooker (1.8L) isn't turning on, or won't switch to "Keep Warm".
A:

Confirm the inner pot is seated flat against the heating plate. If the pot is dented or debris is on the heating plate, the weight sensor will fail to trigger.

If the cooker burns rice or fails to auto-switch to "Keep Warm," the internal thermostat is likely faulty. Do not attempt further use; file a warranty claim.

Safety Warning: If there is any burning smell (electrical, not food) or sparking at the plug, advise the customer to stop use immediately and unplug.

Q: My CG Table Fan High Speed is making grinding noises, wobbling, or won't spin.
A:

Wobbling: Ensure the fan is placed on a completely flat, hard surface.

Won't spin: Check the power outlet. If the motor hums but the blades don't spin, the motor bearings may be seized or the capacitor has failed.

Grinding noise: Unusual grinding typically indicates internal mechanical wear. File a warranty claim if within the warranty period.

3. Books & Media
Q: My Bestseller Book Set of 4 arrived with missing books, damaged covers, or blank/misprinted pages.
A:

Missing books: Ask the customer to list exactly which titles from the 4-pack (Atomic Habits, Psychology of Money, Subtle Art, Rich Dad Poor Dad) are missing, and arrange for a replacement of the missing items.

Misprints: Blank pages or upside-down printing are manufacturing defects. Issue a replacement for the specific defective book.

Transit damage: If the box and book spines are crushed, log this as a shipping issue and process a replacement.

4. FMCG, Groceries & Dairy (Quality Issues)
Note: For consumables, do not troubleshoot. Focus on refund/replacement protocols based on safety and expiry.

Q: My DDC Standard Milk (1 Liter) is spoiled, curdled upon boiling, or the packet was leaking.
A:

Ask the customer for the expiration date and batch number printed on the packet.

Since milk is a highly perishable daily supply, issue an immediate refund or replacement credit if reported within 24 hours of delivery.

Log leaking packets as a transit/handling defect.

Q: My Wai Wai Chicken Noodles or Kurkure Masala Munch packets are puffed up, open, or stale.
A:

Puffed packets (Kurkure): Normal due to nitrogen flushing to keep chips crisp. Not a defect unless the seal is broken.

Broken seal/stale (Wai Wai/Kurkure): If the packet arrived open or the product is stale despite being within the expiry date, process a replacement or refund.

Escalation Triggers
Escalate to the human technical support / warranty team immediately if:

Safety Hazards: Any sparking, electrical burning smell, smoke, or overheating beyond normal operating limits occurs (particularly with the LG Fridge, Baltra Cooker, or CG Fan).

Persistent Hardware Failure: The product fails after all documented, non-safety-risk troubleshooting steps.

DOA (Dead on Arrival): Electronics (Redmi, boAt, Fantech) fail to power on straight out of the box.
