# Technical Support Knowledge Base — Product-Specific Troubleshooting (Part 2)

This document complements the main Technical Support KB (app/login/payment/smart-home issues) with troubleshooting specific to each product category in the catalog. Use this when the customer's issue relates to a physical product malfunction rather than the app/account/payment.

## Kitchen Appliances

**Q: My Electric Kettle 1.5L won't switch on or doesn't auto shut-off.**
A:
1. Confirm the kettle is seated properly on its cordless base (contacts must align).
2. Check the power outlet with another device to rule out a socket issue.
3. Ensure water level is above the minimum fill line — kettles with dry-boil protection may refuse to heat if underfilled.
4. If auto shut-off doesn't trigger when water boils, stop use immediately (safety risk) and file a warranty claim rather than continuing troubleshooting.

**Q: My Air Fryer 4L isn't heating properly or food isn't crisping.**
A:
1. Confirm the temperature dial/display is set correctly and the timer is running.
2. Avoid overcrowding the basket — this is the most common cause of uneven cooking, not a device fault.
3. Check the basket is fully inserted and the safety shut-off isn't triggered by an open drawer.
4. If the heating element shows no glow/heat at max temperature, this is a hardware fault — file a warranty claim.

**Q: My Microwave Oven 20L is making noise, sparking, or not heating.**
A:
1. **Sparking is a safety issue** — advise the customer to stop use immediately, unplug, and not attempt further troubleshooting. This is never a "keep using it" situation. Escalate directly to warranty/repair.
2. If simply not heating (no sparks), confirm the door is closing fully and the door safety latch isn't obstructed.
3. Check if the turntable motor is turning — if not, the turntable coupler may need replacement (covered under warranty).

**Q: My Induction Cooktop 2000W won't detect the cookware or shows an error code.**
A:
1. Confirm the cookware is induction-compatible (magnetic base) — non-compatible cookware is the most common cause of "no pan detected" errors, not a device fault.
2. Center the cookware on the marked cooking zone.
3. Check for an E-series error code on the display and note it for the technician if escalating — error codes usually indicate a specific fault (voltage, overheat, or sensor issue).
4. Ensure adequate ventilation around the unit; overheating protection can trigger shutoffs in enclosed spaces.

**Q: My Mixer Grinder 750W motor is weak, overheating, or making a burning smell.**
A:
1. **Burning smell = stop use immediately** and unplug; this indicates possible motor damage — file a warranty claim, do not continue troubleshooting.
2. Check that jars are locked correctly onto the base (safety interlock prevents operation if misaligned).
3. Avoid continuous use beyond 2-3 minutes per cycle; motor overheating from extended use is a usage issue, not a defect, unless it happens within normal operating limits.

## Large Appliances

**Q: My Refrigerator 250L isn't cooling properly.**
A:
1. Check that the thermostat/temperature dial is set correctly (not accidentally turned to minimum).
2. Ensure the door seal isn't damaged or obstructed by food items preventing a full seal.
3. Confirm adequate clearance (at least 5-10cm) behind and around the unit for ventilation.
4. Check if condenser coils (back of unit) are dusty — recommend cleaning.
5. If cooling issues persist after these checks, this is a compressor/refrigerant issue — file a warranty claim (compressor is covered for 10 years).

**Q: My Washing Machine 7kg won't start, won't drain, or leaks water.**
A:
1. **Won't start**: Confirm door is fully latched (front-load machines lock during cycles and won't start if not sealed), check power supply, confirm water inlet valve is open.
2. **Won't drain**: Check for a clogged drain filter (usually accessible at the bottom front of the unit) — this is a user-clearable issue, not a fault.
3. **Leaking**: Check inlet/outlet hose connections are tight; if leaking from the door during a cycle, check for an obstruction preventing full door seal.
4. If error codes display, note the code for the technician when escalating.

## Climate Control

**Q: My Split AC 1.5 Ton isn't cooling / has an error code / has water leaking from indoor unit.**
A:
1. Confirm the temperature and mode settings on the remote (not accidentally set to fan-only mode).
2. Check/clean the air filter — a clogged filter is the most common cause of reduced cooling and is user-serviceable.
3. Water leakage from the indoor unit usually indicates a blocked drain pipe — this typically requires a technician visit.
4. Error codes on the display should be noted and shared during escalation, as they map to specific fault codes (compressor, sensor, or gas-related).
5. If cooling is weak but not absent, this may indicate low refrigerant, requiring a technician visit rather than user troubleshooting.

**Q: My Tower Fan / Ceiling Fan is making noise, wobbling, or has reduced airflow.**
A:
1. For ceiling fans, wobbling usually indicates blades need re-balancing or mounting screws have loosened — recommend a technician check rather than customer self-repair (safety risk with ceiling-mounted equipment).
2. For tower fans, check for obstructions in the intake/outtake vents and clean the exterior grille.
3. Unusual grinding noise in either product typically indicates motor bearing wear — file a warranty claim if within warranty period.

**Q: My Room Heater isn't heating or shuts off unexpectedly.**
A:
1. Check if the tip-over safety switch has been triggered (heater must be sitting fully upright and level).
2. Confirm the room isn't triggering the overheat cutoff (used in enclosed, poorly ventilated spaces).
3. If the heating element shows no glow at all when powered on, this is a hardware fault — file a warranty claim.

**Q: My Air Purifier's air quality indicator isn't changing or airflow feels weak.**
A:
1. Check the filter replacement indicator — HEPA filters typically need replacement every 6-8 months depending on usage; a clogged filter causes reduced airflow and is a consumable, not a warranty issue.
2. Ensure intake and outtake vents are unobstructed by furniture or curtains.
3. If the air quality sensor shows no reading at all (blank/frozen), this may be a sensor fault — escalate as a technical issue.

## Entertainment & Audio

**Q: My LED TV 43-inch has no picture, no sound, or won't turn on.**
A:
1. Confirm the power cable and wall outlet are working (test with another device).
2. Try a different HDMI port/cable if the issue is with a connected source device rather than the TV itself.
3. For "no sound only" issues, check if a soundbar/external speaker is connected via HDMI ARC and may have taken over audio output — check TV audio output settings.
4. For a completely blank/black screen with power light on, try a factory reset via remote (Settings > System > Reset) — but note down any error message first.

**Q: My Soundbar / Bluetooth Speaker won't pair, cuts out, or has no sound.**
A:
1. For Bluetooth pairing failures: ensure the speaker is in pairing mode (usually a blinking blue LED), forget/remove the device from the phone's Bluetooth list and re-pair from scratch.
2. For soundbars connected via HDMI ARC: confirm the TV's ARC/eARC setting is enabled and using the correct HDMI port (usually labeled "ARC").
3. Audio cutting out intermittently over Bluetooth is often caused by distance/interference — recommend staying within 5-8 meters with no major obstructions.
4. Persistent no-sound issues after all steps should be escalated with the exact connection method (Bluetooth/HDMI/optical) used.

**Q: My Wireless Earbuds or Over-Ear Headphones have connection drops, low battery life, or one side not working.**
A:
1. Update firmware via the companion app if available.
2. Reset earbuds: place both in the case, hold the pairing button 10+ seconds, then re-pair.
3. **One side not working**: Confirm both earbuds are charging fully in the case (check charging pins for dirt/lint) — this is the most common cause, not a hardware fault.
4. Reduced battery life significantly below spec (e.g., less than half the stated hours) after normal charging cycles may indicate battery degradation — file a warranty claim if within the warranty period.

## Mobile & Wearables

**Q: My smartphone is running slow, overheating, or battery drains fast.**
A:
1. Check for pending OS/app updates.
2. Close background apps and restart the device.
3. Check battery health in device settings; batteries degrading below 80% capacity within the warranty period may qualify for a warranty claim.
4. Overheating during charging is often linked to using a non-original or incompatible charger — recommend using the original wall charger/cable.

**Q: My Fitness Band isn't tracking steps/heart rate accurately or won't charge.**
A:
1. Ensure the band is worn snugly (loose fit affects heart rate sensor accuracy) — this is a wearing issue, not a defect.
2. Clean the charging contacts and try an alternate USB port/charger.
3. Restart by holding the side button 10+ seconds if the device becomes unresponsive.
4. If tracking is wildly inaccurate despite correct wear, or the device won't hold a charge at all, escalate as a hardware fault.

## Computing & Accessories

**Q: My Laptop 15.6-inch won't turn on, freezes, or won't charge.**
A:
1. Try a different power outlet and confirm the charging LED (if present) lights up when plugged in.
2. Hold the power button for 10-15 seconds to force a restart if frozen.
3. If the charging light doesn't activate with the original charger on multiple outlets, this points to a charger or port fault — escalate for warranty diagnosis.
4. Do NOT open the laptop chassis or attempt battery replacement — this voids warranty coverage.

**Q: My Wireless Mouse or Mechanical Keyboard isn't responding or has connection drops.**
A:
1. Check/replace batteries (wireless mouse) or confirm the USB receiver is plugged directly into a USB port (not through a hub, which can cause dropouts).
2. Re-pair via Bluetooth if using Bluetooth mode: remove device from paired list and pair again.
3. For keyboards with unresponsive individual keys, this typically indicates a hardware switch fault — file a warranty claim rather than attempting DIY repair.

**Q: My Power Bank 20000mAh isn't charging devices or won't charge itself.**
A:
1. Test with a different cable and device to rule out a cable/device-side issue.
2. Confirm the power bank's LED indicators light up when connected to a charger — no LED activity at all suggests internal battery/circuit failure.
3. Power banks that feel unusually hot during charging should be discontinued from use immediately and reported — this is a safety concern, not routine troubleshooting.

**Q: My USB-C Cable or Wall Charger isn't charging my device / charges very slowly.**
A:
1. Test the cable/charger with a different device to isolate whether the fault is the cable, charger, or the original device.
2. Confirm the device supports the charger's wattage — slow charging with a 65W charger on a phone that only supports 18W input is expected behavior, not a fault.
3. Frayed cables or chargers that spark, smell burnt, or feel hot should be discarded immediately, not repaired or continued in use.

## Storage & Networking

**Q: My External Hard Drive 1TB or Pen Drive 64GB isn't detected by my computer.**
A:
1. Try a different USB port and a different computer if possible to isolate the fault.
2. Check Disk Management (Windows) or Disk Utility (Mac) to see if the drive appears but isn't mounted — this may indicate a formatting/driver issue rather than hardware failure.
3. If the drive isn't detected on any port/computer and shows no activity light, this points to hardware failure — file a warranty claim (note: data recovery is not covered under standard warranty).

**Q: My Wi-Fi Router has weak signal, frequent disconnects, or won't let me log into the admin panel.**
A:
1. Restart the router (unplug 30 seconds, plug back in).
2. Check placement — routers work best in open, central, elevated locations away from thick walls and metal objects.
3. For frequent disconnects, check if too many devices are connected simultaneously (may exceed router capacity).
4. If the router won't allow login to the admin page even after a factory reset (reset button held 10 seconds), this indicates a hardware fault.

## Electrical & Power Backup

**Q: My Extension Board's sockets aren't working or the surge protector light is off.**
A:
1. Test each socket individually with a known-working device — partial socket failure is common and isolated to that socket, not the whole unit.
2. If the surge protection indicator light is off, the surge protection function may have already tripped from a past power spike; the board may still supply power but without surge protection — recommend replacement.
3. **Any burning smell, discoloration, or heat from the extension board is a fire safety risk** — advise immediate disconnection and replacement, not repair.

**Q: My Inverter with Battery isn't switching to backup power during an outage.**
A:
1. Confirm the inverter is switched to "Auto" mode, not "Off" or "Charge only."
2. Check the battery water level (for non-sealed/tubular batteries) and top up with distilled water if low — this is routine maintenance, not a defect.
3. If the inverter shows a fully charged battery indicator but still fails to supply backup power, this indicates an internal fault — file a warranty/repair claim.

## Additional Home Appliances

**Q: My Water Purifier (RO) has slow flow, bad taste/odor, or a filter warning light.**
A:
1. A filter warning light means the filter needs replacement (consumable, not covered under warranty) — typically every 6-12 months depending on water quality and usage.
2. Slow flow rate is commonly caused by a clogged pre-filter or low input water pressure — check the water source pressure first.
3. Bad taste/odor after a recent filter change can indicate the system needs a flush cycle (run 2-3 liters through before regular use).

**Q: My Robot Vacuum Cleaner won't dock, loses connection, or stops mid-clean.**
A:
1. Ensure charging dock is against a wall with 1.5m clear space on either side and no reflective/glass surfaces nearby (can interfere with sensors).
2. Clean the charging contacts on both the robot and dock.
3. Update the companion app and firmware.
4. If the vacuum repeatedly stops on certain floor types/thresholds, check for obstacles or exposed wires it can't sense — this is often an environmental factor, not a fault.

**Q: My Handheld Vacuum Cleaner has weak suction or won't turn on.**
A:
1. Empty the dust cup and check/clean or replace the filter — a full dust cup or clogged filter is the most common cause of weak suction.
2. Check for blockages in the intake nozzle.
3. For cordless models, confirm battery is charged; for corded models, test the outlet.

**Q: My Iron Box (Steam) isn't producing steam, is leaking water, or is sticking to fabric.**
A:
1. Confirm the water tank is filled above the minimum line and the steam setting is enabled (not set to dry-iron only).
2. Water leaking from the soleplate during use at low temperatures is expected on some fabric settings — check temperature dial matches fabric type.
3. Sticking to fabric is usually due to a dirty soleplate — recommend a self-clean cycle or damp-cloth wipe when cool.

## Personal Care

**Q: My Hair Dryer, Trimmer, or Electric Toothbrush isn't turning on or holding charge.**
A:
1. For corded hair dryers: test the outlet and check for a tripped built-in thermal cutoff (common after continuous long use — let it cool 15-20 minutes).
2. For cordless trimmers/toothbrushes: confirm charging contacts are clean and dry, and try a full charge cycle (60-90+ minutes) before concluding it's faulty.
3. Rapid battery drain within the warranty period (e.g., holding charge for only a few minutes) qualifies for a warranty claim.

**Q: My Digital Weighing Scale shows inconsistent or inaccurate readings.**
A:
1. Ensure the scale is on a hard, flat, level surface — carpet or uneven flooring causes inaccurate readings and is not a device fault.
2. Replace the battery if the display appears dim or flickers.
3. Recalibrate per the product manual if available (usually a step-on-then-off sequence).

## Furniture & Lighting (Setup/Quality Issues)

**Q: My Study Table Lamp or LED Ceiling Light flickers, won't turn on, or has uneven brightness settings.**
A:
1. Confirm the lamp/light is fully connected to power and any touch-dimmer function isn't stuck on a low setting.
2. Flickering at specific brightness levels (not all) may indicate a dimmer compatibility issue rather than a full product fault.
3. Complete failure to power on after checking the outlet indicates a hardware fault — file a warranty claim.

**Q: My furniture (Sofa, Dining Table, Bed Frame, Wardrobe, Bookshelf, Office Chair) arrived with an assembly issue, missing parts, or a wobble/instability problem.**
A:
1. Confirm all parts and hardware (screws, bolts, connectors) were used per the assembly manual — missing pieces from packaging should be reported for a free replacement part, not treated as a return.
2. Wobbling is often due to uneven flooring or under-tightened bolts — advise re-checking all connection points are fully tightened.
3. If a structural defect is present (cracked wood, bent metal frame) independent of assembly, this qualifies as a manufacturing defect — route to Return & Refund (for large appliances/furniture, replacement-only window) rather than general technical troubleshooting.
4. Office Chair hydraulic/height adjustment failing to hold position indicates a gas lift cylinder fault — file a warranty claim.

## Escalation Trigger for This Document
Escalate to human technical support / warranty team if:
- Any safety-relevant symptom appears: sparking, burning smell, overheating beyond normal, exposed wiring.
- Product fails after all documented, non-safety-risk troubleshooting steps.
- Issue is confirmed to be a manufacturing/hardware defect rather than a usage or setup issue.
- Cross-reference the **Warranty & Repair** document once a hardware fault is confirmed, to determine repair vs. replacement eligibility.
