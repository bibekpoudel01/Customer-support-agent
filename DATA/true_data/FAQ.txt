E-Commerce Marketplace Customer Support Knowledge Base

Account, Registration, and Login
New customers can create an account using their email address or mobile number, and can also sign up quickly through a linked Google, Facebook, or Apple account for faster onboarding. Login is supported through password, one-time password (OTP) sent to the registered mobile number or email, or biometric login on the mobile app for supported devices. If a customer forgets their password, they can reset it through the "Forgot Password" link, which sends a reset link or OTP to their registered email or phone number; for security, password reset links typically expire after a short window and can only be used once. Customers can update their name, email, phone number, date of birth, and profile picture from the Account Settings or "My Profile" section, though changing the registered email or phone number may require OTP verification on both the old and new contact details to prevent unauthorized account takeover. Multiple saved addresses can be added, edited, or removed from the address book, with one address markable as the default. Customers can also link or delink payment methods, manage saved cards, and view stored UPI IDs from the same settings area. If an account is compromised or a customer notices unfamiliar login activity, they should immediately reset their password, review recent orders, and contact support to lock the account temporarily. Accounts inactive for a long period are generally not deleted automatically, but customers who wish to permanently delete their account and associated data can request account deletion through settings or support, which is usually processed within a stated number of business days in line with data protection regulations, after which order history and saved details are permanently removed.

Browsing, Search, Wishlist, and Personalization
Customers can search for products using keywords, brand names, categories, or by scanning a barcode or image through the app's visual search feature. Search results can be filtered by price range, brand, customer rating, discount percentage, delivery speed, and other category-specific attributes such as size, color, or storage capacity, and sorted by relevance, price, newest arrivals, or popularity. Items of interest can be saved to a Wishlist for later purchase, moved directly to cart from the wishlist, or shared with friends and family via a shareable link. The platform also maintains a "recently viewed" and personalized recommendation feed based on browsing and purchase history, which customers can reset or opt out of from their privacy settings if they prefer not to have personalized recommendations. Price-drop and back-in-stock alerts can be enabled on individual product pages so that customers are notified by push notification, SMS, or email when a wishlisted or out-of-stock item becomes available or drops in price.

Placing, Tracking, and Managing Orders
To place an order, a customer adds items to the cart, selects a delivery address, chooses a payment method, and confirms the order, after which an order confirmation with an order ID is sent via email, SMS, and app notification. Orders can be tracked in real time from the "My Orders" or "Order History" section, which shows current status such as order confirmed, packed, shipped, out for delivery, or delivered, along with an estimated delivery date and, where available, a live courier tracking link or delivery partner contact number. Multiple items in a single order may ship separately from different warehouses or sellers, in which case each item or shipment gets its own tracking ID and delivery estimate, and the order page reflects each sub-shipment's status independently. Customers can modify an order — including changing the delivery address, adjusting quantity, or removing an item — only during a short window before the order is packed for shipment; once packed or shipped, changes are no longer possible and the customer must instead wait for delivery and initiate a return, replacement, or exchange afterward. Order invoices, including GST or tax invoices where applicable, are available for download from the order details page for both personal and business record-keeping.

Order Cancellation
Orders, or individual items within a multi-item order, can typically be cancelled free of charge any time before the item has been shipped, by selecting "Cancel" on the order details page and choosing a reason. Once an item has shipped, cancellation is usually no longer available through self-service, and the customer instead needs to either refuse delivery at the doorstep or accept delivery and initiate a return afterward. If a prepaid order is cancelled before shipping, the full amount is automatically refunded to the original payment method or store wallet within a stated number of business days; cash-on-delivery orders that are cancelled before dispatch do not involve any charge. Sellers or the platform may occasionally cancel an order due to stock unavailability, pricing errors, delivery-area restrictions, or failed quality checks, in which case the customer is notified and any amount paid is refunded automatically without requiring further action.

Payments, Wallets, and Pricing
The platform accepts a wide range of payment methods including credit cards, debit cards, net banking, UPI, mobile wallets, and cash on delivery (COD) where available for the delivery pincode and order value. No-cost or standard EMI options may be offered on eligible cards and order values above a minimum threshold, with EMI tenure and interest terms displayed at checkout before confirmation. A store wallet or store credit balance, once credited from a refund, cancellation, or promotional cashback, can be applied automatically or manually at checkout to reduce the amount charged to the primary payment method. If a payment is deducted from a customer's bank account or card but the order does not show as confirmed on the platform, this is usually a temporary sync delay or a failed transaction on the payment gateway side; such amounts are auto-reversed to the original payment method within a standard number of business days, and customers are advised not to reattempt payment until the status is confirmed to avoid double charges. Coupons, bank offers, and promotional discounts are applied automatically at checkout when eligibility conditions such as minimum order value, specific bank card, or category restrictions are met, and only one coupon can generally be combined with one bank offer per order unless stated otherwise. Prices, including any applicable taxes and delivery charges, are shown transparently at checkout before payment confirmation, and price changes after an order is placed do not retroactively affect an already-confirmed order.

Shipping, Delivery, and Delivery Issues
Standard delivery timelines vary by product, seller location, and delivery pincode, typically ranging from same-day or next-day delivery in serviceable metro areas to three to seven business days elsewhere, with express or scheduled delivery slots available for an additional fee on eligible items. Customers can choose a preferred delivery date or time slot for select categories such as large appliances or furniture, and can also opt for "leave at doorstep," "hand it to a specific person," or "call before delivery" preferences where the delivery partner's app supports it. If a package shows as delivered but the customer has not received it, they should first check with neighbors, building security, or other household members, then report the issue through the "Where's My Order" or "I haven't received my item" option within the stated claim window so an investigation can be opened with the delivery courier; confirmed lost-in-transit or missing-after-delivery-scan cases are typically resolved with a full refund or replacement. Damaged, tampered, or incorrect items received should be reported immediately with photos through the order's "Report an Issue" flow, which triggers a pickup for the wrong or damaged item alongside a replacement or refund, without requiring the customer to pay return shipping in most such cases. International or cross-border orders, where offered, may include customs duties, import taxes, and longer delivery windows that are disclosed at checkout, and such orders may have different return eligibility due to cross-border logistics constraints.

Returns, Refunds, Replacements, and Exchanges
Return eligibility windows vary by category, commonly ranging from seven to thirty days from delivery, and are always shown on the individual product page before purchase since perishable goods, innerwear, personal care items, made-to-order products, and certain electronics are frequently marked as non-returnable or replacement-only due to hygiene or customization reasons. To initiate a return, exchange, or replacement, the customer selects the relevant order and item, chooses a reason (wrong item, damaged, defective, doesn't fit, changed mind, etc.), and selects a resolution type where offered: refund, replacement with the same item, or exchange for a different size or variant. Most returns include free doorstep pickup by a delivery partner; in categories or locations where pickup isn't available, the customer may be asked to self-ship the item using a provided prepaid shipping label, with the shipping cost reimbursed as part of the refund. Refunds for prepaid orders are issued to the original payment method or, for faster processing, to the store wallet, while refunds for cash-on-delivery orders are issued via bank transfer, UPI, or store wallet since there is no original payment instrument to refund to; refund timelines typically range from same-day wallet credit to five to ten business days for bank or card reversals depending on the bank's processing time. Items must generally be returned unused, in original packaging, with all tags, accessories, manuals, and freebies included for the return to be accepted; returns missing these elements may be rejected or subject to a partial refund. Replacement requests are typically limited to one per order per item and are subject to stock availability of the same product; if the item is out of stock, the customer is offered a full refund instead. Products covered under a brand or manufacturer warranty that develop a fault after the return window has closed are directed to the manufacturer's authorized service center, with the platform providing warranty card details, service center locators, or a request pickup for brands that offer at-home service pickup.

Marketplace, Sellers, and Product Authenticity
As a marketplace connecting third-party sellers to customers, individual product listings may be fulfilled either directly by the platform's own warehouses or by independent sellers, and both delivery timelines and return policies can vary slightly by seller, which is disclosed on the product page under seller information and seller ratings. Customers can view a seller's rating, total ratings count, and years active on the platform before purchasing, and can message a seller directly for pre-purchase questions on certain listings. All products sold on the platform are expected to be genuine and unused unless clearly listed as refurbished or open-box, and refurbished listings must disclose their condition grade and refurbishment warranty separately from the manufacturer's original warranty. If a customer suspects a counterfeit, fake, or significantly-not-as-described item, they can report it through "Report a Problem" on the order, which typically triggers an automatic full refund and removal of the listing pending seller investigation, independent of the seller's own return policy. Disputes between a customer and a seller over item condition, description accuracy, or fulfillment delays are typically mediated by platform customer support, which has authority to force a refund or replacement outside of standard seller-set policies when a genuine seller-side error is confirmed.

Gift Cards, Vouchers, and Store Credit
Digital gift cards and vouchers can typically be purchased in fixed or custom denominations and delivered instantly via email or SMS to the recipient, or scheduled for a future delivery date such as a birthday or holiday. Gift card balances can be applied at checkout alongside other payment methods, and any unused balance remains in the account for future orders until the card's expiry date, if one applies. Store credit issued from a refund, cancellation, price-adjustment, or goodwill compensation functions similarly and is usually non-transferable and non-withdrawable to a bank account, existing solely as in-platform purchasing balance unless local regulation requires cash refund options.

Membership, Loyalty, and Subscription Programs
Paid membership or loyalty programs, where offered, typically bundle benefits such as free or expedited shipping on eligible orders, early access to sales, exclusive member-only deals and pricing, extended return windows, and access to bundled streaming or content services, billed either monthly or annually with the option to cancel auto-renewal at any time from account settings. Free loyalty or rewards point programs let customers earn points on qualifying purchases, reviews, or referrals, redeemable as a discount or store credit on future orders, with point expiry policies and earning rates disclosed in the program's terms.

Customer Support and Complaint Escalation
Customer support is generally available through in-app chat, a help-center ticketing system, phone support during stated business hours, and social media channels for urgent public escalations. Most order-specific issues — tracking, cancellation, return, refund status, or damaged-item claims — can be self-served directly from the order details page without needing to contact an agent, which is usually faster than a support ticket. If an issue remains unresolved after initial contact, customers can request escalation to a senior support representative or a formal complaint reference number, and if still unsatisfied, may be directed to the platform's grievance officer or a consumer dispute resolution forum in line with local consumer protection law. Response time commitments — such as first response within 24 hours and resolution within a stated number of business days — are usually published in the platform's help center or terms of service.

Reviews and Ratings
Only customers who have purchased a product through the platform are generally permitted to leave a verified rating and written review, which helps maintain review authenticity; reviews can include photos or videos of the actual received product. Reviews found to violate guidelines — such as containing offensive language, personal information, competitor promotion, or being clearly fraudulent or paid — can be reported and are subject to removal after moderation. Sellers may respond publicly to reviews to address concerns, and customers can edit or delete their own review at any time after posting.

Security, Privacy, and Fraud Prevention
The platform uses encrypted connections and does not store full card numbers on its own servers, relying instead on PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway tokenization for saved cards. Customers are advised never to share OTPs, passwords, or full card details with anyone claiming to be platform support over phone or messaging apps, since the platform never asks for OTPs to process a refund, cancel an order, or "verify" an account. Suspicious order confirmation emails, fake delivery-fee payment links, or unsolicited calls asking for payment to "release" a package are common phishing tactics and should be reported directly through the official help center rather than acted upon. Personal data collected — including browsing history, purchase history, and location — is governed by the platform's privacy policy, and customers can typically request a copy of their stored data or request deletion in accordance with applicable data protection law.

Business, Bulk, and Corporate Orders
Businesses or bulk buyers can typically register for a business account offering GST-compliant tax invoicing, bulk-order quotes, credit-line or purchase-order payment options, and dedicated account management for large or recurring orders, distinct from the standard consumer checkout flow. Bulk discount eligibility, minimum order quantities, and lead times vary by category and are best confirmed directly with the business sales team or bulk-order request form rather than assumed from standard listing prices.

App, Website, and Technical Issues
If the app or website is running slowly, failing to load product images, or showing a payment error, customers are generally advised to check their internet connection, update to the latest app version, clear the app cache, or try an alternate browser before contacting support, since most such issues are resolved by these basic steps. Persistent technical errors — such as a payment being charged without an order being created, an inability to log in despite correct credentials, or a broken tracking link — should be reported with a screenshot and device/app version details to expedite technical support diagnosis.

Accessibility and Special Assistance
The platform aims to support customers with disabilities through screen-reader-compatible app and website design, alternative text on product images, and voice-search or voice-command ordering support where available. Customers requiring special delivery assistance, such as help carrying heavy items indoors or additional time to receive delivery at the door, can note this in delivery instructions or contact support in advance to coordinate with the delivery partner.

General Platform Questions, Trust, and Value Proposition
Customers often ask why they should choose this platform over other online stores or a local shop, and the honest answer is a combination of factors rather than any single one: a wide selection across categories in one place, competitive and transparently displayed pricing, verified seller and product quality checks, buyer-protection policies on returns and refunds, multiple secure payment options including cash on delivery, and reliable delivery tracking, all backed by a dedicated customer support system — the platform does not claim to always be the cheapest option on every single item, since prices are set by individual sellers and can vary, but it does aim to offer strong overall value, transparent policies, and a trustworthy buying experience. When asked what the website actually provides, the short answer is that it operates as an online shopping marketplace offering product discovery and search, secure checkout and multiple payment methods, order tracking, doorstep delivery, returns and refunds, customer support, and account-based features like wishlists and order history — it does not manufacture products itself in most cases, but connects customers to its own retail arm and/or verified third-party sellers who list and fulfill the products. A common question is whether any items on the platform are given away for free — the answer is no, the platform does not give away free products; what customers may see instead are free delivery on orders above a certain value, free gifts or samples bundled with specific promotional purchases (clearly marked as a limited-time offer tied to buying an actual product), or free trial periods on paid subscription services, but there is no general "free item" giveaway independent of an actual purchase. Customers sometimes ask if signing up or having a membership is required to shop — it is not required for browsing or even for most checkout flows, since guest checkout is available, though creating a free account and/or subscribing to a paid membership unlocks additional benefits like faster checkout, order history, and exclusive deals. On the question of whether the platform sells only new products, the answer is that the overwhelming majority of listings are new and unused, but select categories may include clearly labeled "refurbished," "open box," or "renewed" items sold at a discount with their own specific warranty terms, and these are never presented as new. Customers asking whether prices shown are final or negotiable are told that all prices, including taxes and any delivery charges, are shown transparently at checkout and are fixed as displayed — there is no in-app price negotiation, though coupons, bank offers, and sale events can lower the effective price. A frequent question is how often the platform runs sales or discount events — big sale events are typically seasonal or tied to specific calendar occasions and are announced in advance through the app, email, and notifications, while smaller day-to-day deals and clearance discounts run continuously on rotating products. Customers ask whether the platform is safe to use for payments — yes, all payment processing goes through PCI-DSS compliant, encrypted gateways, and the platform itself does not store full card numbers. On authenticity, customers frequently ask if all products are guaranteed genuine — the platform states that it takes counterfeit prevention seriously through seller verification and quality checks, and offers a full refund if a customer receives a proven counterfeit item, though as a marketplace with many independent sellers it cannot claim zero possibility of an isolated bad listing slipping through, which is why the reporting and refund mechanism exists as a safety net. Customers ask if there's a minimum order value required to shop — generally no minimum is required to place an order, though a minimum order value may apply specifically to qualify for free delivery or for certain coupon codes. Some ask whether the platform ships to every location in the country — delivery coverage is extensive but not universal, since certain remote or restricted pincodes may have limited serviceability, longer delivery times, or cash-on-delivery unavailability, which is always shown at checkout before order confirmation based on the entered address. Customers ask whether they can shop and pay in a different country's currency — this depends on whether the platform operates internationally; where cross-border shopping is supported, currency conversion and applicable import duties are shown clearly before checkout. On sustainability, customers sometimes ask if the platform offers eco-friendly packaging or carbon-neutral delivery — where such initiatives exist, they are usually opt-in or automatically applied on select orders and disclosed on the relevant product or checkout page rather than being a blanket guarantee across every single order. Customers occasionally ask if the platform has a referral program — where offered, referral programs reward both the existing customer and the new customer with a discount, credit, or bonus once the referred person completes their first qualifying purchase, with exact terms available on the referral page. Finally, customers often ask how the platform differs from simply buying directly from a brand's own website — the key differences are the convenience of comparing multiple brands and sellers in one place, unified order tracking and returns across all purchases regardless of brand, and platform-wide buyer protection policies that apply uniformly rather than varying by each individual brand's own return policy.
